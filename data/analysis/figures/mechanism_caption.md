## Why the last sentence of the long caption is there

α is a **chosen** parameter, not a measurement: the injection adds `α·d̂` with
α = 1.0·‖R‖ = 30.07, so its displacement along `d` is 30.07 by construction. The
prefix positions are measured from the residual. Same units, same quantity, but one
is a knob and one is an observation, and a careful reader will ask.
